Evidence ledger

Every fact is stored with the exact wording it came from and checked back against your document. Anything the extraction could not find verbatim is marked and excluded from drafting, so a paraphrase can never become a new claim.

Where to look, and what to type

Queries are derived from the ledger, not invented. This page cannot browse the web, so it hands you pre-filled searches on the boards that matter in Singapore — and a config file for the repository workflow that pulls MyCareersFuture listings for real.

Listings and how you actually match them

Add a posting by pasting it, or load listings.json produced by the repository workflow. Each requirement is matched against ledger evidence; unmet requirements are reported as gaps rather than quietly covered over.

Drafts with their sources attached

Claude may re-order, re-weight and re-word what is in the ledger. It may not add an employer, title, date, tool, metric or outcome that is not there. Both a deterministic audit and a second reading check the draft against the ledger before you see it.
